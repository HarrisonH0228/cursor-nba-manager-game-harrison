Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

NBA Manager does not require an account. We do not sell your personal information.

What we store

  • A browser session cookie with your selected team and active season ID.
  • Season save files on the server (rosters, standings, game state) linked to your session.
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, user agent) if hosted on a cloud provider.

Third parties

We load fonts and scripts from CDNs (Google Fonts, Bootstrap) and team logos from NBA CDN. Those providers may collect technical data per their own policies.

Your choices

Starting a new game clears your team assignment. Clearing browser cookies removes your session. Season files may remain on the server until overwritten.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the repository or hosting provider listed with this deployment.
